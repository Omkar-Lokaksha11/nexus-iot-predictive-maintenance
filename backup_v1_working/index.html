DEPARTMENT OF MECHANICAL ENGINEERING • MDM MINI PROJECT

SmartTwin AI: Digital Twin for Smart Manufacturing

An AI-driven Digital Twin combining continuous physical machine telemetry simulation with machine learning predictive maintenance to detect tool wear, thermal dissipation failures, and mechanical overstrain before catastrophic plant breakdown occurs.

1. Problem Statement & Motivation

Traditional industrial maintenance relies on either reactive maintenance ("run until failure") or rigid preventative maintenance (fixed time intervals). Both approaches lead to either expensive catastrophic machine damage or premature replacement of functioning tooling.

SmartTwin AI bridges this gap by creating an active software Digital Twin that continuously tracks thermal gradients, rotational speeds, and tool fatigue to predict failure probability with trained machine learning.

2. The AI4I 2020 Dataset & Features

The system is trained on the official UCI AI4I 2020 Predictive Maintenance Dataset using 6 validated industrial features:

  • Type: Machine quality variant (L: 50%, M: 30%, H: 20%).
  • Air Temperature [K]: Ambient temperature in Kelvin.
  • Process Temperature [K]: Internal machine temperature in Kelvin.
  • Rotational Speed [rpm]: Spindle motor velocity.
  • Torque [Nm]: Cutting force torque output.
  • Tool Wear [min]: Cumulative insert cutting time.

3. Failure Modes Diagnosed

  • Tool Wear Failure (TWF): Critical flank wear (≥200 min).
  • Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF): Low thermal gradient (ΔT < 8.6 K) at low airflow speeds.
  • Power Failure (PWF): Mechanical power outside safe 3,500 W - 9,000 W envelope.
  • Overstrain Failure (OSF): Product of torque and wear exceeding metallurgical limits.

4. Limitations & Future Scope

  • Simulation Boundaries: Telemetry is generated via physics simulation; physical deployment requires microcontrollers (e.g. ESP32, Modbus).
  • Vibration Segregation: Vibration is simulated for visualization and rule checks, but correctly excluded from the ML pipeline as the AI4I dataset lacks vibration.
  • Future Enhancements: Remaining Useful Life (RUL) regression, vibration FFT spectrum analysis, and OPC-UA industrial protocol support.
