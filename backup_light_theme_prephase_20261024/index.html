1. Problem Statement & Motivation

Traditional manufacturing maintenance relies on reactive "run-to-failure" or calendar preventative replacements. Reactive approaches risk costly spindle damage ($10k+), while calendar replacements discard functioning cutting tools prematurely.

SmartTwin AI eliminates this dilemma by predicting failure probabilities dynamically from real-time operational stresses.

2. The AI4I 2020 Dataset & Features

Trained exclusively on the official UCI AI4I 2020 dataset (10,000 synthetic records):

  • Type: Variant grade (L: 50%, M: 30%, H: 20%).
  • Air & Process Temp: Ambient & internal spindle temps in Kelvin.
  • Rotational Speed: 1,168 - 2,886 RPM.
  • Torque: 3.8 - 76.6 Nm.
  • Tool Wear: Cumulative insert wear minutes.

3. Mechanical Failure Modes

  • Tool Wear Failure (TWF): Flank insert wear exceeds 200 - 240 minutes.
  • Heat Dissipation Failure (HDF): Temp delta ΔT < 8.6 K at rotational speeds < 1,380 RPM.
  • Power Failure (PWF): Mechanical spindle power falls outside 3,500 W - 9,000 W.
  • Overstrain Failure (OSF): Product of torque and wear exceeds metallurgical shear limit.

4. Academic Boundaries & Scope

  • Simulation Boundaries: Software emulation twin running locally on Windows. Physical deployment would connect accelerometers via Modbus/MQTT.
  • Vibration Segregation: Verified data science integrity: vibration is separated from the ML model as the AI4I dataset contains no vibration.
  • Future Roadmap: Remaining Useful Life (RUL) regression pipeline, vibration FFT spectrum, and OPC-UA PLC industrial gateway.
